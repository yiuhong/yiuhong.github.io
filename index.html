Yiu Hong Ko, Ph.D., RSW

Cognitive neuroscientist at Hong Kong Metropolitan University. My research focuses on metacognition, decision confidence, error processing, individual differences, and aging. I use electroencephalography (EEG) and perceptual decision-making tasks to investigate the neural and cognitive mechanisms of decision making. I am also a Registered Social Worker (RSW) in Hong Kong. Currently serving as a journal reviewer and welcome invitations from journals, books, and conferences.

Besides research, I also enjoy teaching and creating teaching materials with animation and hands-on examples. And I share useful resources here.

Current Research

My primary research investigates metacognitive decisions. Currently, I am working on various projects on metacognition in education, metacognition in older adults, and metacognition in daily life situations.

I also explore areas including dense longitudinal neuroimaging (DLN), functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS), and individual differences.
